## Direction contract

THESIS: A mission control dashboard for internet health. Warm industrial aesthetic with amber on charcoal. Refuses the default AI SaaS look (cream + serif, neon + dark, editorial hairlines). The design commits to a control-room feel: structured, information-dense, warm but serious.

OWN-WORLD: Deep warm charcoal (#1a1410) background with amber (#f0a030) as the sole accent. Bricolage Grotesque for display, JetBrains Mono for labels and data. Sharp corners (6px radius), full borders, no side-tab stripes, no glow effects. Cards are flat panels with 3px left accent bars. Typography is dense and purposeful.

STORY: The visitor sees one blunt answer ("is the internet okay?") in the first viewport, with a thermometer showing alert level. Scrolling reveals only troubled services, sorted by severity. Search finds any service. The design says "this is a tool, not a toy" — serious about data, light about commentary.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Large "is the internet okay?" headline (Bricolage Grotesque 800, 40-96px) left-aligned, occupying 2/3 width. Right side has a vertical thermometer showing alert level 1-5. Below the headline: alert box with level caption and summary. Amber accent on charcoal. No hero image, no gradient text, no decorative elements.

FORM: Warm industrial mission control. Ordered list position: 3 (structured, information-dense, warm palette). SEED REFERENCE: none (pinned — user requested complete redesign with warm industrial aesthetic).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
